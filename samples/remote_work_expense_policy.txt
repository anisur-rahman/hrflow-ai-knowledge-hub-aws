Eligible employees may request reimbursement for approved home office equipment.
Page 2: Equipment
The maximum annual home office equipment allowance is $500. Eligible purchases include an ergonomic desk chair, monitor, keyboard, and other preapproved items. Manager approval is required before purchase.
Page 3: Receipts
Employees must submit itemized receipts within 30 calendar days of purchase. Reimbursement requests must be submitted through the expense portal. Internet reimbursement is limited to $40 per month for approved remote employees.
